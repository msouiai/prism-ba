native measurements before the first score: original observations and original
plain L2 targets, independent endpoint audit, fixed source/binary/input hashes,
N=3 compatibility and practical cells, N=5 tails. No learned components, menu,
Krylov reuse or restoration search. A separate best-state export for the
nonmonotone acceptance arm preserves the minimum actually visited; copying and
storage are timed. Record current-cost rho independently of its reference-cost
acceptance ratio. Only the explicitly named acceptance arm changes the
controller's acceptance predicate. Every other direction retains Eta2's
controller and identifies any temporary objective/stopping-stage transitions.

The native executable and all flags are frozen per registered experiment.
`off` uses the identical derived executable with intervention switches absent.
No CPU compaction/heavy forensic analysis overlaps scored timings. Diagnostic
captures and derivative tests have separate records and are excluded from
performance claims. Full endpoint data stays on durable disk after verified
lossless compression; compact evidence is committed separately from large data.
