# Brief 2 chart witness pre-test

Registered before any BAL chart comparison. This is conditional point solving at fixed captured cameras, not a solver rollout or a target-time claim. The frozen champion and scored objective remain unchanged.

Inputs are the nine already selected Brief0 captures: Venice0/1/2, Final3068 0/5/6, Ladybug1197 outer1 0/1/2. Main camera direction is the captured Eta2 clipped proposal; secondary comparison uses the accurate reference's clipped camera direction. Do not substitute a camera direction chosen by chart outcome. Read exact native matrix states and original observations with their hashes.

For each state/camera direction, compare Euclidean, normalized homogeneous S3 with Householder tangent, and frozen-anchor inverse depth. Anchors are the first original-order observation of each point; no per-scene choices or anchor updates during the step. All point systems use consistent FP64 Jacobians, the inherited lambda, tau=lambda, and the same relative-diagonal/floor construction in their respective chart. Chart-coordinate diagonal damping changes the physical damping metric; the result is not a pure-retraction ablation. Retain the native compact point direction as an additional reported reference.

Run three repetitions per cell with one CPU thread, charging diagnostic assembly/solve/scoring separately. No claim about GPU overhead follows from these CPU times. Report score_init, true full cost/decrease, full GN prediction and rho, relative cost delta versus Euclidean, point-equation residual, maximum Euclidean displacement, fling count relative to max camera-center distance from their mean, invalid projections, and cheirality flips. Infinity is scored in homogeneous coordinates when the projection is finite; undefined projections remain infinite cost, not dropped observations. Include point/camera/cross signed and absolute model errors and the top200 point error concentration. Apply the source-clipped residual-certification caveat from Brief0.

Primary mechanism gate: a chart must improve true candidate cost by >0.15% on at least two distinct primary states across at least two scene families, with positive predicted decrease and rho>0.1 there, and no >0.15% regression on another primary state, to justify an always-on native rollout. Separately report weaker or local improvements. A possible guarded rollout requires a separately registered global guard; do not silently tune per witness. If historical flings are absent in the frozen Ladybug control, report that mechanism as absent rather than claiming their removal or declaring a 0-to-0 improvement.

Do not run the tau sweep or depth-freezing rollout merely because the chart is representable: their motivation requires this pre-test and the full Brief0 error decomposition. S3 bounded angle and inverse-depth regularity do not prove bounded Euclidean displacement, cheirality correctness, or safe tau<<lambda. Failed numerical point solves remain failed diagnostics.
