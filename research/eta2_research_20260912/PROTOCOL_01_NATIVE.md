# Brief 1 registration: a late additive coarse level

Registered after the witness/coarse/spectral pre-tests and before any native additive-coarse rollout. The coherent Venice0 camera spectrum and the independently CPU-completed full direction support this test. They do not predict a speed result for the mixed-storage production operator. The latter must be validated separately.

One global rule: use K=8 geometric camera clusters and the additive inverse `BJ^-1 + Z (Z^T A_lambda Z)^-1 Z^T`. Activate at the first attempt after at least eight accepted outers for which the last accepted relative objective decrease is below 1e-3 and the current lambda is below 1e-3. Once activated, keep it active. This identifies weakly damped slow progress using already available past information; no scene-name rule, endpoint knowledge, or predictor training. Retain ordinary Eta2 residual forcing, clipping, acceptance, rescues, stopping and numerical repair.

Choose deterministic geometric clusters at activation, with K capped by ncam. Use the registered native Sim(3) tangent and CG scaling, intrinsics zero. Recompute the basis geometry with the current cameras and scaling; memberships stay fixed. Remove zero/dependent columns explicitly, normalize retained columns, and report effective rank. Intrinsic regularization, the actual compact cross fragments, point factors, camera normal blocks and scaling are the production values. This experiment does not silently replace the operator with the FP64 reference.

Assemble the coarse matrix from local camera contributions and point-cluster interactions, without a dense AZ allocation. Count initialization, clustering, transfers, per-attempt assembly, dense factorization and every coarse application inside native wall. Coupled point damping requires rebuilding on each changed-lambda attempt. No cache or Krylov reuse across attempts. A failed coarse SPD/finiteness/parity check falls back to the original BJ preconditioner for the entire attempt, logs the failure, and adds no new damping floor or outcome-specific tuning.

Correctness first: verify the pinned source/44 headers; tiny BAL under sanitizer; compare explicit sparse coarse assembly to streamed `Z^T A Z` from the unchanged production product; check symmetry and positive preconditioner energy; same-derived-binary off arm plus original compatibility N=3. The expensive streamed construction is a correctness oracle only, not the deployed implementation. If parity fails, fix the implementation before timing.

Then compare original, same-binary off, and coarse on with the common campaign harness. Primary practical panel is the frozen nine cells: Ladybug539, Trafalgar138, Final394 at 1.005/1.01/1.02 times their existing anchors, N=3; targets and input hashes are copied from the existing frozen calibration, not recalibrated. Native caps remain 5/5/8 seconds respectively. Tail panel: Venice52 target243740.27 and Final3068 target1744796.9841897595, N=5, 60 native seconds/600 outers with ordinary FTOL. Alternate run order and retain every miss, score_init, endpoint audit, PCG/outer, rejects, retry-wall and activation/setup counters. If it changes no practical trajectories, report it as an inactive control there.

Do not promote without the standing >0.15% median cost or disjoint target-time ranges, no lost target reliability on the sampled practical cells, and no >20% practical median-time regression. A tail improvement remains local unless transferred. If K8 fails, report this registered implementation as negative; do not claim all K or all two-level methods are refuted. K32/K128, deflated and balanced variants require a separately frozen continuation justified by setup/work measurements. No first-multigrid/deflation-BA novelty claim.
