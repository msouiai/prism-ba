# Brief 5 registration: can an early trajectory predict its endpoint?

Registered before collecting this cohort. This is the predictor pre-test, not a racing or front-loading speed claim. Source and original binary hashes are the frozen Eta2 hashes in PROTOCOL_00.md; all champion flags and CLI remain identical. Only the target stop is disabled to observe the endpoint; native cap is 60 seconds and outer cap 600, with ordinary FTOL active. Score every original observation, k2=0.

Run 20 fresh repetitions each on Ladybug1197 and Final3068, alternating scenes. These are repeated executions exploiting existing solver-local floating-point ordering variability, not twenty controllable RNG seeds. Retain identical outcomes, all caps and failures. Record costs after accepted outers 3, 5 and 10 from native curves, native wall, final independently audited cost, initial score, accepts/rejects, matvecs, and existing radius/rho logs. Do not introduce parameter perturbations after seeing the results.

Primary predictor is cost after accepted outer 5. Report full-cohort Spearman correlation for each checkpoint, scatter plots, and separate correlations for chronological halves 0–9 and 10–19. Missing checkpoints are missing, not extrapolated; exact ties use average ranks. A predictor is usable only if it has at least 16 observed pairs, full-cohort |rho|>=0.4, and both halves have the same sign with |rho|>=0.4. This stricter split check limits optimistic racing based on a noisy in-sample correlation. A constant predictor is undefined and fails the gate.

If the primary fails, predeclared alternatives, in order, are cost3, cost10, mean accepted rho through outer5, rejected-attempt count through outer5, and median raw/clipped camera ratio through outer5. Select an alternative on the first half only by largest absolute correlation among those with |rho|>=0.4; it must pass the same-signed |rho|>=0.4 held-out-half check, as well as the full-cohort threshold. Preserve all alternatives. These are candidate-selection diagnostics, not additional positive outcome claims. No test-cohort feature tuning.

No racing implementation unless a predictor passes on both scenes. Front-loading is a distinct hypothesis and will require its own registered global setting and comparison; a failed predictor alone does not refute that hypothesis. Costs at checkpoints are native trace values; final and initial score parity and exact commands/hashes are retained. Exported endpoints are losslessly compressed after independent FP64 audit and decompressed hash verification. CPU audit, loading and export are excluded from native timing and reported separately. No cross-attempt or cross-outer Krylov reuse.
